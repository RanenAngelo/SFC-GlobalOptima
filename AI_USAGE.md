# AI Usage Declaration

**Project:** DineIQ Analytics
**Team:** Global Optima
**Competition:** TechWiz 7, Data Science Intelligence Arena
**Institution:** Aptech Limited

## 1. AI Tool Used

During the development of DineIQ Analytics, the team used **Claude** as an AI assistance tool.

AI was used only for limited parts of the project and was not used to develop the entire system.

## 2. Areas of AI Usage

### User Interface

Claude was used to provide limited assistance with a small portion of the User Interface. The suggestions were reviewed and adapted by the team to fit the design and requirements of DineIQ Analytics.

### Dataset Generation

Claude was also used as an assistance tool during the development of the data generation process. It helped with ideas and approaches for generating the synthetic restaurant data required for the project.

The generated data and data generation process were reviewed by the team to ensure that they were suitable for the project requirements.

## 3. Human Review and Modification

All AI assisted output used in the project was reviewed by the team.

The team modified and adapted AI generated suggestions where necessary rather than using them without review.

The final implementation was tested by the team to ensure that it worked with the DineIQ Analytics project and followed the requirements defined in the SRS.

## 4. Human Responsibility

We acknowledge that Claude was used during the development of DineIQ Analytics for limited assistance with a portion of the User Interface and the synthetic data generation process.
